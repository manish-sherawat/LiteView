1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

At LiteView, we believe that personal documents, camera scans, and notes should remain private. LiteView is engineered from the ground up to operate completely offline without relying on cloud servers or external data pipelines.

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, document content, or usage data to third parties.

2. Information Collection & Data Use

Because LiteView functions entirely on your local Android device hardware, our data collection practices are minimal:

  • No Personal Identification: LiteView does not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or user profiles.
  • No Document Content Access: Your PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Text files are parsed and rendered strictly inside your device's memory.
  • No Network Tracking: LiteView contains zero third-party analytics frameworks, advertising trackers, or telemetry beacons.

3. Device Permissions Explained

To view and scan documents on your phone, LiteView requires specific Android system permissions:

Storage & Document Access

Used strictly to locate, read, and display documents stored in your device memory or SD card.

Camera Access

Used exclusively when you trigger the HD Document Scanner feature to capture paper files into local PDFs.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All annotations, highlights, sticky notes, signatures, or scanned documents created within LiteView remain stored locally in your phone's sandbox or public Downloads folder. You retain complete control to move, back up, or delete your files at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

LiteView does not target or collect data from individuals under 13 years of age. Our application contains no user accounts or data collection features for any age group.
