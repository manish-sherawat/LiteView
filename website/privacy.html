PRIVACY POLICY

Technical data protection guidelines for LiteView. All document parsing, rendering, and camera operations execute locally on your physical Android hardware with zero cloud telemetry.

EFFECTIVE DATE: AUGUST 2026 VERSION 2.5.3

01. OVERVIEW & PRIVACY COMMITMENT

LiteView is engineered as an air-gapped, zero-telemetry utility application for Android. We believe personal documents, digital notes, and camera scans must never be accessible to unauthorized remote services.

LiteView does not collect, record, transmit, monetize, or profile any personal information, usage metrics, or document contents.

02. DATA COLLECTION ARCHITECTURE

Because LiteView functions entirely within the local Android OS sandbox without external cloud connections:

  • NO USER IDENTITY TRACKING No accounts, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers are ever required or stored.
  • NO CLOUD UPLOADS All PDF, Word, Excel, and Text files are rendered strictly in volatile device memory and local storage.
  • ZERO TELEMETRY BEACONS The application includes zero third-party advertising frameworks, analytics SDKs, or background pinging routines.

03. ANDROID SYSTEM PERMISSIONS SPECIFICATION

To execute document rendering and optical camera scanning, LiteView requests only minimal, necessary Android system permissions:

  • STORAGE & FILE ACCESS (READ/WRITE) Used strictly to discover, open, and save document files located on your internal flash storage or SD card.
  • CAMERA ACCESS (HARDWARE SENSOR) Used exclusively during active HD Document Scanner capture sessions to convert physical documents to local PDFs.

04. LOCAL DATA RETENTION & CONTROL

All user annotations, highlights, digital signatures, bookmarks, and scans created in LiteView are stored exclusively on your device. You retain 100% control to edit, backup, export, or permanently delete any file at any time.
