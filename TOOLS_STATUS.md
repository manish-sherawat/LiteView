Working (all offline):

1. Image to PDF: turn photos into a PDF
2. PDF to Images: save PDF pages or embedded pictures as images
3. Word to PDF: convert .docx files to PDF
4. PowerPoint to PDF: convert .pptx and .ppt slides into PDF
5. Excel to PDF: convert .xlsx, .xls, .xlsm, and .csv spreadsheets to PDF
6. Text to PDF: convert plain text, notes, code, and markdown into PDF
7. Rotate Pages: rotate individual or all pages in 90° increments
8. Protect PDF: encrypt & lock PDF with password and custom permissions
9. Repair PDF: diagnose, rebuild cross-reference table, and recover corrupted pages
10. Merge PDFs: join PDFs into one
11. Extract Pages: pull chosen pages into a new PDF
12. Page Reorder: drag pages into a new order
13. Watermark PDF: stamp text on every page
14. Compress PDF: make the file smaller
15. Sign PDF: add a drawn, typed, or uploaded signature
